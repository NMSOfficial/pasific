# Staged exam grading

Teacher flow:

1. Define exam metadata and scoring rules.
2. Upload the blank exam (required) and an answer key (optional).
3. Upload filled student papers and verify student matching.
4. Pasific performs OCR, creates the attempt, and starts AI grading in one server request.
5. AI compares the blank exam, optional answer key, and student OCR transcript.
6. The result remains hidden until teacher review and approval.

General grading defaults intentionally accept semantically equivalent wording and reasonable partial credit unless teacher scoring rules or the exam explicitly require an exact form.
